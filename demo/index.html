TINY FPS

A tiny multiplayer parkour shooter in the browser. Open this page in another tab to join the other team.

Game mode

CTF Capture the flag Two teams, two flags. Take the enemy flag from its base and run it home while your own flag is there. First team to 3 captures wins, or the one ahead after 8 minutes.

More modes are coming.

Map

Mouse to look · WASD (ZQSD) to move · Shift sprint · Space jump, twice in the air · a direction twice to dash · C crouch or slide · E rover · Esc for every key
