# Weapon

The user's direction, 2026-11-06: a first gun, taken near the spawn. It is inspired by the Halo plasma pistol: a curved alien sidearm, violet metal with cyan glow strips. It fires lasers at a high rate, with an ammo counter and recoil. Players have 5 HP, and 5 hits put a player down. Health packs are spread over the map and give 2 HP each. A hit plays a hit sound to the shooter, as in Quake 3. Everything stays procedural, with no assets. The client budget is raised to 48 KB (the user, 2026-11-06).

## Rules

| Rule         | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Health       | `HP_MAX` 5, a hit takes 1. No friendly fire. No regeneration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Gun          | Taken at the team's rack (`TeamDef.rack`, within `RACK_R`) with `AMMO_MAX` 60 shots. The rack fills on arrival only: stepping into the circle loads `AMMO_MAX` once, staying gives nothing more, and leaving and coming back loads it again (`PlayerState.rack` tells he is inside, on the wire). Lost when going down. `ammo` is -1 without it. |
| Fire         | Hold `KEY.FIRE`: one hitscan laser every `GUN_COOLDOWN` (7) ticks, from the eye along the look, `GUN_RANGE` 80 m, stopped by the brushes (`ray`). The host checks hits on the others as the shooter saw them (`Room.rewind`).                                                                                                                    |
| Recoil       | A view kick of `RECOIL` up per shot, on the client. The kick is in the pitch the inputs carry, so the host aims where the player sees. Sustained fire climbs.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Down         | At 0 HP the player lies down for `DOWN_TICKS` (2 s) and cannot move or fire. A carried flag drops where he fell, with the tackle's 2 s lock. Then he is back at a team spawn with full health and no gun.                                                                                                                                        |
| Health packs | `WorldDef.packs`, up to 32. Within `PACK_R` a pack gives `PACK_HP` (2) up to `HP_MAX`; a player at full health does not take it. It is back after `PACK_BACK` (20 s). `Snapshot.packs` holds the bits of the packs that are there.                                                                                                               |
| Tackle       | Unchanged; it works with or without the gun.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

The gun state (`hp`, `ammo`, `cool`, `down`, `shots`, `hits`) is in `PlayerState`. A shared step fires it from the inputs, so the client predicts its own shots and ammo exactly. Hits and damage are the host's. The `shots` and `hits` counters (u8, wrapping) go on the wire, so every client draws the others' lasers, and the shooter hears a hit when its `hits` changes.

## Fixes after the first play (the user, 2026-11-06)

- The gun is aligned with the shot: its barrel points where the laser goes, and the laser leaves its muzzle.
- The gun's top is smooth, and it shows the ammo count on its back, glowing digits facing the player.
- Ammo packs are spread over the map (`WorldDef.ammo`): within `PACK_R` a player with the gun and fewer than `AMMO_MAX` shots takes `AMMO_PACK` (20) shots, up to `AMMO_MAX`. A player without the gun does not take it. It is back after `PACK_BACK`. Their bits follow the health packs' in `Snapshot.packs`.

## The electric katana (the user, 2026-11-06)

A second weapon: a katana with a cyan electric glow along the blade. Its cut depends on the direction key held: a thrust, a cut across or a slash from above. The client budget is raised to 56 KB for it (the user, 2026-11-06).

| Rule    | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pickup  | Taken at the team's katana rack (`TeamDef.blade`, within `RACK_R`), next to the gun rack. No ammo. Lost on going down, with the gun.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| In hand | A player carries both. `KEY.BLADE` says which one he wants in hand: 1 the gun, 2 the katana, the wheel swaps. Without the gun the katana is in hand whatever the bit says, and it stays in hand through a swing, so a cut that started always strikes. `PlayerState.wield` is the result of the step.                                                                     |
| Cuts    | FIRE with the katana in hand starts a cut when the last swing is over (`swing` 0), unless the hands are busy (a hang, a climb). LEFT or RIGHT alone cuts across from that side, else FORWARD thrusts, else it slashes down. The numbers per cut are in `CUTS`: thrust 2 HP, 2.4 m, a narrow line, 0.4 s; across 2 HP, 1.9 m, a wide arc, 0.5 s; slash 3 HP, 1.9 m, 0.6 s. |
| Hit     | Host only, on the strike tick, against the enemies as the attacker saw them (`Room.rewind`). Every enemy in the cut's reach and arc is hit, and the attacker's `hits` goes up once.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Shock   | A hit that does not put the enemy down shocks him: a stumble of `SHOCK_TICKS` (0.4 s), thrown `SHOCK_KNOCK` the way the blade goes (a thrust and a slash along the attacker's look, a cut across toward the side it ends on). `zap` tells the client it is a shock, not a tackle: no tackle banner. It does not drop a carried flag; going down does.                     |
| On wire | `blade`, `wield`, `zap` in the top 3 bits of the gun field (bits 21, 22, 23). One byte more per player (46): bits 0-5 `swing`, bits 6-7 `cut`. The input keys take 10 bits.                                                                                                                                                                                               |

`gun.ts` `step()` runs the katana with the gun: the rack, the choice of the weapon in hand, the swing timer. It returns which weapon struck on this step, so the host resolves a shot or a cut. Both are predicted by the client exactly; the hits and the shock are the host's. `step()` returns `STRIKE.NONE`, `STRIKE.SHOT` or `STRIKE.CUT` (gun.ts); `blade.ts` has `cutOf(keys)`, `strike(world, attacker, foes)` and `knock(attacker)`.
