# Weapon

The user's direction, 2026-11-06: a first gun, taken near the spawn. It is inspired by the Halo plasma pistol: a curved alien sidearm, violet metal with cyan glow strips. It fires lasers at a high rate, with an ammo counter and recoil. Players have 5 HP, and 5 hits put a player down. Health packs are spread over the map and give 2 HP each. A hit plays a hit sound to the shooter, as in Quake 3. Everything stays procedural, with no assets. The client budget is raised to 48 KB (the user, 2026-11-06).

## Rules

| Rule         | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Health       | `HP_MAX` 5, a hit takes 1. No friendly fire. No regeneration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Gun          | Taken at the team's rack (`TeamDef.rack`, within `RACK_R`) with `AMMO_MAX` 60 shots. The rack fills on arrival only: stepping into the circle loads `AMMO_MAX` once, staying gives nothing more, and leaving and coming back loads it again (`PlayerState.rack` tells he is inside, on the wire). Lost when going down. `ammo` is -1 without it. |
| Fire         | Hold `KEY.FIRE`: one plasma ball every `GUN_COOLDOWN` (7) ticks, from the eye along the look at `PLASMA_SPEED` (40 m/s), `GUN_RANGE` 80 m. It bursts on the first brush or enemy body it touches (see Plasma balls below).                                                                                                                       |
| Recoil       | A view kick of `RECOIL` up per shot, on the client. The kick is in the pitch the inputs carry, so the host aims where the player sees. Sustained fire climbs.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Down         | At 0 HP the player lies down for `DOWN_TICKS` (2 s) and cannot move or fire. A carried flag drops where he fell, with the tackle's 2 s lock. Then he is back at a team spawn with full health and no gun.                                                                                                                                        |
| Health packs | `WorldDef.packs`, up to 32. Within `PACK_R` a pack gives `PACK_HP` (2) up to `HP_MAX`; a player at full health does not take it. It is back after `PACK_BACK` (20 s). `Snapshot.packs` holds the bits of the packs that are there.                                                                                                               |
| Tackle       | Unchanged; it works with or without the gun.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

The gun state (`hp`, `ammo`, `cool`, `down`, `shots`, `hits`) is in `PlayerState`. A shared step fires it from the inputs, so the client predicts its own shots and ammo exactly. Hits and damage are the host's. The `shots` and `hits` counters (u8, wrapping) go on the wire, so every client flashes the others' muzzles, and the shooter hears a hit when its `hits` changes.

## The electric katana (the user, 2026-11-06)

A second weapon: a katana with a cyan electric glow along the blade. Its cut depends on the direction key held: a thrust, a cut across or a slash from above. The client budget is raised to 56 KB for it (the user, 2026-11-06).

| Rule    | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pickup  | Taken at the team's katana rack (`TeamDef.blade`, within `RACK_R`), next to the gun rack. No ammo. Lost on going down, with the gun.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| In hand | A player carries both. `KEY.BLADE` says which one he wants in hand: 1 the gun, 2 the katana, the wheel swaps. Without the gun the katana is in hand whatever the bit says, and it stays in hand through a swing, so a cut that started always strikes. `PlayerState.wield` is the result of the step.                                                                     |
| Cuts    | FIRE with the katana in hand starts a cut when the last swing is over (`swing` 0), unless the hands are busy (a hang, a climb). LEFT or RIGHT alone cuts across from that side, else FORWARD thrusts, else it slashes down. The numbers per cut are in `CUTS`: thrust 2 HP, 2.4 m, a narrow line, 0.4 s; across 2 HP, 1.9 m, a wide arc, 0.5 s; slash 3 HP, 1.9 m, 0.6 s. |
| Hit     | Host only, on the strike tick, against the enemies as the attacker saw them (`Room.rewind`). Every enemy in the cut's reach and arc is hit, and the attacker's `hits` goes up once.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Shock   | A hit that does not put the enemy down shocks him: a stumble of `SHOCK_TICKS` (0.4 s), thrown `SHOCK_KNOCK` the way the blade goes (a thrust and a slash along the attacker's look, a cut across toward the side it ends on). `zap` tells the client it is a shock, not a tackle: no tackle banner. It does not drop a carried flag; going down does.                     |
| On wire | `blade`, `wield`, `zap` in the top 3 bits of the gun field (bits 21, 22, 23). One byte more per player (46): bits 0-5 `swing`, bits 6-7 `cut`. The input keys take 10 bits.                                                                                                                                                                                               |

`gun.ts` `step()` runs the katana with the gun: the rack, the choice of the weapon in hand, the swing timer. It returns which weapon struck on this step, so the host resolves a shot or a cut. Both are predicted by the client exactly; the hits and the shock are the host's. `step()` returns `STRIKE.NONE`, `STRIKE.SHOT` or `STRIKE.CUT` (gun.ts); `blade.ts` has `cutOf(keys)`, `strike(world, attacker, foes)` and `knock(attacker)`.

## The hold, like Jedi Knight 2 (the user, 2026-11-07)

The user found the katana badly held and the arms twisted, and pointed at Jedi Knight 2's first-person saber fights. Measured before the fix: the wrist turned up to 127° against the forearm and bent up to 171°, and the elbow twisted up to 177°. The hands were placed from the blade alone, both with the right hand's turn, so the mirrored left hand held the grip backwards, and the arm only swung the shortest way to them.

- `wield()` in `body.ts` places one arm on the grip for both the own body and the runners. The hand comes first: its axis leaves the handle on the shoulder's side, the thumb along the handle. The elbow goes where the forearm carries on that axis, and it is a hinge. The forearm's turn about itself is split half at the elbow, half at the wrist.
- The guard holds the hands low, at the bottom of the view, the blade leaning forward: more blade than hands (the user, 2026-11-07).
- The cuts swing the whole arms (the user, 2026-11-07: "the moves start from the wrist only"). The hands go round a wide arc with the blade pointing out along it: over the left shoulder, across the middle and out low to the right for the cut from the left (40 to 55 cm of travel), above the head and down to the bottom of the view for the slash, back to the right hip and out for the thrust. The cut from the right is written apart, shorter on its wind-up, where the left arm reaches across the chest. Every key keeps both grips within 0.6 m of their shoulders, so the arms reach them standing and running.

## Plasma balls (the user, 2026-11-06)

The gun stops being a hitscan laser: like Doom's plasma gun it fires glowing balls that fly, so a shot is led and can be dodged. Its sound gets much deeper, like the Doom 3 plasma rifle.

| Rule      | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shot      | Unchanged trigger (`GUN_COOLDOWN`, ammo, `shots`, recoil). Each shot is a ball from the eye along the look at `PLASMA_SPEED` (40 m/s), radius `PLASMA_R`, for `GUN_RANGE` m.                                                                                                                                            |
| Hit       | The host moves every ball each tick as a swept sphere: it bursts on the first brush (`ray`) or enemy body (a cylinder of `PLAYER_R` + `PLASMA_R` and the body's height) on its way that tick. An enemy hit takes 1 hp and counts in the shooter's `hits`. Teammates, the shooter and players lying down let it through. |
| Lag       | A ball starts where the shooter saw it start: the host advances a new ball by the shooter's lag (the input's view tick to now, at most 12 ticks), testing the brushes and the others as they are now over that stretch.                                                                                                 |
| Wire      | The snapshot carries the balls in flight, at most `PLASMA_MAX`: an id (u8, wraps), the shooter's id, where it is and where it goes, and one last time with a burst flag where it burst, so that every client draws the burst.                                                                                           |
| Own balls | The shooter's client spawns its own ball at once when its predicted step fires and flies it itself; the host's copy of it is used only for its burst.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Look      | A blue-white glowing ball with a soft halo and a short trail, lighting what it passes if the renderer can afford it; a bright splash of light and sparks where it bursts. The muzzle flashes blue.                                                                                                                      |
| Sound     | A plasma shot that falls from the treble to the bass, a square and a saw a few hertz apart for a flanger's sweep over a short thump (the user, 2026-11-07, after a deep Doom 3 style one), and a wet electric splash at the burst, placed in the world.                                                                 |

Done: `plasma.ts` (`launch()`, `fly()`: the swept sphere against the brushes, `ray()` grown by `PLASMA_R`, and the enemy cylinders), the balls in `Room` (lag capped at `PLASMA_LAG` 12 ticks, flown against the others as they are now; a ball is named by its shooter's id and his `shots` after the shot), 10 bytes a ball after the flags (a count byte, only when any flies), a burst sent once more since every snapshot reaches every peer. The client (`balls.ts`) flies its own balls from the predicted shot along the host's line and to the same wall, takes the host's copy only for a burst short of the wall, and draws the others' INTERP_DELAY late, eased out of the muzzle in 0.12 s. `beams.ts` draws them, the blue flash and the splash with sparks and lights; the laser beam is gone. The burst plays `SOUND.ZAP` until `SOUND.SPLASH` and the deep shot land in `sfx.ts`.
